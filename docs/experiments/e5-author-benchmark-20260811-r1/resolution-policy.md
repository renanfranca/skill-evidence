# Blind review resolution policy

Two reviewers judge independently before seeing one another's decisions, condition identity, expected lifecycle, schedule, or aggregate scores. Their initial judgments are immutable.

The resolver reviews only explicit disagreements or `NEEDS_ADJUDICATION` judgments after both submissions are locked. Resolution must cite an atomic reference item and observable `SKILL.md` or `context.md` evidence, accept semantic alternatives, and record a concrete rationale. Mechanical schema, lifecycle, controlled-field, packet-blindness, schedule, reservation, provenance, and reference-integrity failures are not overridable. Any material change to these instructions, the probes, reference items, or this policy after collection invalidates the campaign.
