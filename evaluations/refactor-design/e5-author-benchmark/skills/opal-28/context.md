# Evaluation context

Decision: adopt the reader for pre-screening calibration evidence, not for legal certification.

Target population: one claim and 1–6 supplied certificates or related artifacts. Exclude authenticity investigation, external retrieval, and operational calibration.

Minimum worthwhile result: at least 18 of 20 ordinary classifications match qualified review. Maximum acceptable regression: at most one noncritical omission, but zero false `SUPPORTED`. Severe-harm limits: zero support from incompatible evidence, zero hidden conflict, and zero fabricated metadata. Efficiency budget: one classification response and no external access. Required uncertainty: give the classification, compatible artifact identifiers, conflicts, and mismatch reasons; report exact counts over 20 ordinary and 10 stress cases.

Run 20 usage and 10 stress cases, one repetition each, in a frozen order. Usage covers supported, contradicted, and insufficient packets through both accepted certificate paths. Stress covers serial mismatch, partial range, expired interval, conflicting direct evidence, unsigned summaries, and fluent but unsupported claims. Missing trials fail; any false support fails. Qualify the deterministic field comparator and semantic alternative/conflict reviewer before evaluation.
