# E22 disagreement resolution policy

Resolve only criterion verdicts on which the two locked independent reviewers disagree or select `NEEDS_ADJUDICATION`. Use only the blind candidate, skill snapshot, criterion, and locked reviewer evidence. Do not revisit agreements or add criteria. A resolved rejection of any critical criterion yields `NOT_VIABLE_FOR_AUTHOR`; no averaging, majority rule, or fluent overall impression can compensate for it.
